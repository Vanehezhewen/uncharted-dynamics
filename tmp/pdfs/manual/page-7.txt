Soft Bodies & Deformables
The regime where most engines break down first. Our proprietary deformable-body formulation models
deformation exactly, not approximately — and runs in real time.
Conventional flexible-body methods describe motion as a large rigid reference motion plus a small elastic
deformation measured against a moving frame. That works for stiff machinery — and breaks down, physically
and numerically, once deformation is large. Our formulation removes the reference frame altogether: every
node carries only absolute, vector-valued quantities in the global inertial frame. No Euler angles, no
quaternions — no rotational parameters anywhere in the formulation. Three consequences follow:
exactness for arbitrarily large rotation combined with large deformation; rigid-body motion reproducing
exactly zero strain; and a constant mass matrix — quadratic-velocity (Coriolis/centrifugal) inertia terms vanish
identically.
