Modeling the Gap
Universal laws are solved and shared. What remains — instance-specific variance — is small, structured, and
generative.
1

Capture real-world noise, oscillations, and residual forces in deployment — from your
hardware, in your task.
2

Learn the structure of the gap: deterministic, parametric, and stochastic components — not a
monolithic correction net.
3
GENERATE
Synthesize realistic noise and oscillation profiles — statistically faithful profiles, not just averages.
4
RECONSTRUCT
Reproduce real-world scenarios for training, validation, and stress testing — including the rare
events that never recur on demand in the lab.
